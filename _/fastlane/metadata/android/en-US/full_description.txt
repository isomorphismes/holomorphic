Analytic Continuation is a native Android explorer for complex functions and convergence-disc geometry.

The live view uses GPU-rendered phase portraits. You can place and move zeros and poles, inspect overlapping convergence discs, and explore how known singularities constrain the radius around each center.

The Android explorer runs natively on the device. The repository also contains a separate desktop movie-rendering path, but that Python and ManimGL tooling is not part of the Android F-Droid build.
