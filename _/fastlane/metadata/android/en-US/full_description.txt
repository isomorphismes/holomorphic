Holomorphic Random Explorer is a native Android complex-function playground built on Wegert-style domain coloring.

Place and move explicit zeros and poles on the ordinary complex plane while a separate nonvanishing holomorphic factor changes continuously. Small workers propose nearby coefficient directions and score their analytic phase/log-modulus effect before the app accepts a step. The GPU evaluates the accepted field for every pixel and hands the resulting phase and log modulus to the Wegert coloring core.

Pause or resume the motion, edit zeros and poles, and zoom the live portrait. Lasso, overlapping-disc, branch-sheet, and broader Riemann-surface experiments live separately in the Lacunary project. The app runs natively on the device and contains no generated movie bundle or desktop animation runtime.
