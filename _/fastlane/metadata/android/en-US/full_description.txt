Holomorphic Random Explorer is a native Android experiment in continuously changing holomorphic maps.

Three small workers search for bounded, low-disturbance changes to a near-identity holomorphic map. The GPU evaluates the moving field for every pixel. Pause and resume the motion or deform the current map directly.

The app is not yet a germ-transport or branch-tracking implementation of analytic continuation. It runs natively on the device and contains no generated movie bundle or desktop animation runtime.
